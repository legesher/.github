### What happens next

1. Automated checks run on your pull request. If one fails, the details are linked from the check, and we're happy to help you read them.
2. A maintainer reviews your changes. We may suggest edits or ask questions, and we'll work through them with you here.
3. Once everything looks good, a maintainer merges it, and we celebrate. 🎉

### Things that help

- Link the issue this addresses, for example `Fixes #123`.
- Describe what you changed and why, in your own words. Write in whichever language you're most comfortable in.
- If an AI assistant helped, say so in the description. That's welcome here; we just like to know.
